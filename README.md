# PREP_PILOT 

### AI Career Copilot for Placements and Internships

PREP_PILOT is an AI-powered career guidance platform that helps students prepare smarter for internships and placements.

Instead of providing generic advice, PREP_PILOT analyzes a student's target company, career goals, resume, and job requirements to generate personalized preparation strategies, interview guidance, and skill-development roadmaps.

---

## 🌟 Why PREP_PILOT?

Most students face the same challenges:

* Unsure what to study for a target company
* Don't know if their resume is strong enough
* Apply to jobs without understanding missing skills
* Receive rejections without actionable feedback
* Struggle to create a structured preparation plan

PREP_PILOT solves these problems using Large Language Models and intelligent resume-job analysis to act as a personalized AI career mentor.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Company Preparation Assistant

Select a target company, skill level, and role.

PREP_PILOT generates:

* Personalized Preparation Roadmap
* Resume Improvement Suggestions
* Interview Preparation Questions
* Company-Specific Strategy

---

### 📄 Resume Analyzer

Upload your resume and receive:

* ATS-Oriented Feedback
* Resume Enhancement Suggestions
* Strength and Weakness Analysis
* Career Guidance Recommendations

---

### 🔍 Resume vs Job Match Analysis

Provide:

* Resume
* Job Posting Link

The platform automatically:

* Extracts Resume Information
* Scrapes Job Requirements
* Identifies Skill Gaps
* Calculates Candidate Match
* Generates Improvement Suggestions

Output includes:

* Match Score
* Missing Skills
* Resume Recommendations
* Personalized Roadmap
* Interview Questions

---

### 🧠 AI-Powered Interview Preparation

Generate role-specific interview questions based on:

* Target Company
* Job Requirements
* Resume Content
* Career Goals
